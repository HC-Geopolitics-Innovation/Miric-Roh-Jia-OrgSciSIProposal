Its extensive product line includes new and remanufactured parts for domestic and imported vehicles, batteries, tires, mobile electronics, fashion accessories, and various private-label branded items, with a significant portion of tires and batteries supplied by key vendors. Services offered encompass engine diagnosis, tune-ups, alignments, air conditioning, fuel injection, tire installation, and oil changes, though body work is excluded from services provided. The business operates across 36 states and Puerto Rico, with store counts fluctuating between 562 and over 750 locations over the years, comprising Supercenters, smaller Service & Tire Centers, and Pep Express retail-only locations. The company utilizes an "Everyday Low Price" strategy to differentiate itself from competitors, including nationwide chains, regional retailers, and independent merchants, while expanding its digital presence through websites, mobile applications, and online ordering platforms to facilitate same-day delivery and expanded auto parts assortments. Additionally, the firm runs a commercial parts delivery program specifically designed for professional installers and dealers, fulfilling demand for slow-moving items through a hub-and-spoke network managed by Superhubs.
