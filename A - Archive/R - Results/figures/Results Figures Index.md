# Results & Figures Index

This folder contains all evaluation figures and architectural diagrams organized by their corresponding section in the analysis pipeline (`D`, `T1`, `T2`, `T3`, `X1`).

---

## Directory & File Organization

### `D - Data Collection and Processing`
- **`fig_dataset_construction.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.tex`**: The three collection rails of D on one page: GovInfo hearings, the C-SPAN reference abstracts, and the SEC 10-K filings, with the measured counts at each step. Same visual language as the T1, T2, and T3 flowcharts.

### `T - Three Step Text Analysis Pipeline/T1 - Text Summarization`
- **`fig_text_summarization_flowchart.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.tex`**: The text summarization pipeline on one page: transcript to speaker turns to chunks, then the map, collapse, and reduce calls to the hearing summary, with the counts of the production run.
- **`T1.3 - Validation of Summaries/T1.3.2 - Validation - Compare Summaries with C-SPAN Abstracts/fig_cosine_similarity_by_pair_type.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Cosine similarity between a hearing's generated summary and C-SPAN's abstract of the same hearing, of other hearings of the same committee, and of hearings of other committees. Drawn by notebook T1.3.2 from the stored pair table; replaces `cosine_similarity_averages`.

### `T - Three Step Text Analysis Pipeline/T2 - Topic Extraction and Stance Analysis`
- **`fig_topic_extraction_flowchart.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.tex`**: The T2 workflow on one page: Track 1 from the summaries to the codebook and the 26 groups, Track 2 from the legislator turns to the placed stance records, and the aggregation to the support share by Congress, with the measured counts.
- **`T2.3 - Validation of Topics/T2.3.2 - Validation - Compare the Codebook with CAP Codes/fig_cap_crosswalk.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Crosswalk heatmap of the 26 topic groups against CAP's human-coded major topics; each cell the share of the group's hearings under that CAP topic.
- **`T2.4 - Stance by Topic over Time/T2.4.1 - Grouped Stance Volume/fig_select_volume_balance_groups.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Concern and support speech turns for each of the 26 topic groups.
- **`T2.4 - Stance by Topic over Time/T2.4.2 - Privacy Group Stance over Time/fig_privacy_support_share_by_congress_panel_a.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Share of supportive legislator statements by Congress on consumer data privacy legislation; `..._panel_b` for the group's other four topics pooled; `..._both_panels` for the two side by side.
- **`T2.4 - Stance by Topic over Time/T2.4.4 - Privacy Topic Stance Volume/fig_privacy_topic_stance_volume.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Concern and support for each of the five topics of the privacy group.

### `T - Three Step Text Analysis Pipeline/T3 - Topic Relevance and Corporate Disclosure`
- **`fig_topic_relevance_flowchart.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.tex`**: The topic relevance pipeline on one page: the topic side from the statements to the policy summary, the firm side from the 10-K Item 1 sections to the firm-level summary, and their cosine as the firm's score, with the counts of the October 2026 run.
- **`T3.4 - Topic Relevance - Score and Figures/T3.4.2 - Figure - Distribution Overall and by Sector/fig_distribution_policy_summary_firm_summary_cosine_all_firms.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Histogram of the topic relevance score over the 1,058 firms of the all-years sample; `..._information_manufacturing_mining_kde` overlays the kernel densities of three sectors.
- **`T3.4 - Topic Relevance - Score and Figures/T3.4.3 - Figure - Sector Means and Medians/fig_sector_mean_minus_sample_mean_policy_summary_firm_summary_cosine_naics2.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: NAICS two-digit sector mean minus the sample mean with nested 90, 95, and 99 percent intervals; `..._median_..._naics2` for the median.
- **`T3.4 - Topic Relevance - Score and Figures/T3.4.4 - Figure - Selected High and Low Relevance Firms/fig_selected_high_and_low_relevance_firms_by_sector.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: Cases selected by the authors, fourteen high-relevance and four low-relevance firms, score minus the sample mean, information-sector firms highlighted; `fig_selected_firms_from_csv_relevance` is the marked list of 22 firms, the proposal's Figure 3, Panel B.
- **`T3.5 - Running Example [Facebook-Cambridge Analytica Scandal Hearing]/T3.5.1 - Example - From the Facebook Hearing's Topic to the Firms It Concerns/fig_running_example_topic_relevance.pdf`** / **`.png`** / **`.svg`**: The two panels above as one figure, the sectors on the left and the selected firms on the right.
